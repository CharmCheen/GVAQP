# Paper Mainline Decision

`PAPER_MAINLINE_DECISION = WEAK_CURRENTLY`

There is a real, well-controlled conditional observation: materialization choice changes event-level recovery across three independent videos under a fixed sparse semantic trace. However, the effect is almost entirely reproduced by a simple temporal gap constraint, while the initially distinctive negative-barrier and current-K3-extra semantics have no material P0 contribution. The reference relation is itself full-grid K3-defined and model-relative, which leaves a direct circularity/independent-validity attack.

The materialization line is therefore **not rejected**, but it is not yet a publishable main algorithm contribution. The only credible next mainline is to test the simpler continuity finding against a frozen independent/human event-continuity reference. A positive outcome could upgrade the problem/operator story to `PROMISING_BUT_GAPS`; a negative outcome should cause a pivot away from materialization as the paper center.

The P0 runner's automatic `REVISE` label used an implementation that required at least 75% *strict* K3 improvements. The requested research criterion is instead at least 75% `K3 >= K0` plus a strict majority; the recorded data satisfy that direction criterion (`54/54 >=`, `40/54 >`). This discrepancy does not alter raw metrics. It also does not remove the novelty/circularity limitations that justify the present `WEAK_CURRENTLY` paper decision.
