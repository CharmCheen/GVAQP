## Proven positive findings

### E1 — Complete, reproducible multi-video model-relative substrate

- **Finding:** DALI, HANGZHOU, and WUHAN now have a complete `1475/1475` frozen VLM-unit reference; V9 contributes 1454 admissible records and V10 Seal B contributes the exact 21-unit missing set.
- **Evidence:** `outputs/v10_multiseal_reference_v1/REFERENCE_MANIFEST.json`; `CROSS_SEAL_COMPATIBILITY_DECISION.json` reports `PASS` on an outcome-blind 9-unit shadow set.
- **Datasets/videos:** 3 independent driving-video sources: DALI (567), HANGZHOU (561), WUHAN (347).
- **Controlled?** YES for execution provenance, not for human semantics.
- **Replicated?** Cross-seal tensor/raw/parsed agreement 9/9; deterministic reference rebuild.
- **Potential confound:** unit labels and event reference are Qwen model-relative, not human/official ground truth.
- **Research relevance:** HIGH as reproducibility infrastructure; not by itself a paper contribution.

### E2 — Sparse-trace materialization materially changes event F1 in the released V3 setting

- **Finding:** On 54 same-trace K0/K3 pairs (3 videos × 3 deterministic selectors × 6 query budgets), K3 is strictly better in 40, equal in 14, worse in 0. Mean ΔF1 is `+0.1840` (bootstrap 95% CI `+0.1405,+0.2286`); median is `+0.1457`.
- **Evidence:** `outputs/p0_materializer_validation_v3/{EXPERIMENT_PROTOCOL.json,trace_identity_checks.csv,materializer_effects.csv,pooled_summary.json}`. All 54 trace checks are `VALID_CONTROLLED_PAIR`.
- **Datasets/videos:** DALI/HANGZHOU/WUHAN; one frozen driving-response query.
- **Controlled?** YES: candidate universe, selected IDs/order, oracle outcomes, call count, and matcher are identical within every K0/K3 pair.
- **Replicated?** Across all three independent sources and three selector families; no stochastic seed variation because selectors are deterministic.
- **Potential confound:** the full-grid reference EventRelation is generated by frozen current-V3 K3 grouping, creating a qualified reference-construction interaction.
- **Research relevance:** HIGH as a conditional mechanism result.

### E3 — The observed P0 effect survives stricter boundary matching

- **Finding:** At the predeclared sensitivity matcher (`tIoU > 0.30`), mean K3−K0 ΔF1 is `+0.1946`, median `+0.1522`; 40 pairs improve and 14 tie.
- **Evidence:** `outputs/p0_materializer_validation_v3/event_metrics.csv`, fields `sensitivity_F1_tiou_0_30` and `sensitivity_TP_tiou_0_30`.
- **Controlled?** YES, same traces as E2.
- **Potential confound:** it remains a K3-defined model-relative event reference; stricter matching alone does not remove circularity.
- **Research relevance:** MEDIUM: rules out a pure overlap-any boundary artifact, but not reference-definition alignment.

### E4 — The main materialization increment is a temporal continuity/gap constraint

- **Finding:** On exactly the P0 traces, historical natural lineage C0→C1 (all-positive span → 10-second gap-limited grouping) yields mean ΔF1 `+0.1738`, median `+0.1377`, positive/equal/negative `40/14/0`. This accounts for nearly all K0→K3 improvement.
- **Evidence:** `outputs/p0_materializer_mechanism_ablation_v1/{MECHANISM_PROTOCOL.json,mechanism_effects.csv,mechanism_summary.csv}`.
- **Controlled?** YES for trace and metric; explanatory post-P0 replay. It adds no VLM calls, selector changes, or parameter sweep.
- **Potential confound:** C1 is deliberately simple and has obvious temporal-coalescing lineage; this weakens any claim that full K3 itself is the novel algorithm.
- **Research relevance:** HIGH: it changes the scientific interpretation of P0.

## Negative findings

### N1 — Queried-negative barrier is not the dominant P0 mechanism

- **Finding:** C2→C3 (adding queried-negative hard barriers) has mean ΔF1 `+0.0034`, median `0`, and changes only 5/54 cells. The P0 traces do not support elevating negative-barrier semantics as the core empirical mechanism.
- **Evidence:** `outputs/p0_materializer_mechanism_ablation_v1/mechanism_summary.csv`.
- **Controlled?** YES, same trace/exact historical C0–C3 lineage.
- **Potential confound:** sparse traces may not often place a queried negative between mergeable positive anchors; zero effect here is not a universal impossibility result.
- **Research relevance:** HIGH; rules out the originally tempting main story under this evidence.

### N2 — Current V3 extras beyond C3 do not improve this P0 primary metric

- **Finding:** C3→current K3 has mean ΔF1 `−0.00034`, median `0`, positive/equal/negative `0/50/4`.
- **Evidence:** `outputs/p0_materializer_mechanism_ablation_v1/mechanism_summary.csv`.
- **Potential confound:** limited unknown/parse-failure support: only one full-grid unknown and zero parse failures in the released reference.
- **Research relevance:** HIGH: full K3 should be described as an implementation/semantics contract, not a separately demonstrated performance gain.

### N3 — MAB/controller mainline is rejected from repository evidence

- **Finding:** 18 one-step physical alternatives have 0 direct event-utility gains; 7 continuations yield only one timing-only conversion; cached contextual policies are near random and worse than fixed always-VERIFY. The process has delayed reward and endogenous/sleeping actions, so it is not a clean MAB.
- **Evidence:** `MAB_RESEARCH_DIRECTION_DECISION.md`; `outputs/public_state_predictability_cached_v3/SUMMARY.json`; `outputs/binary_smdp_value_v1/reports/ORACLE_HEADROOM_REPORT.md`.
- **Controlled?** PARTIAL/targeted, not a universal impossibility theorem.
- **Potential confound:** selected probe states and limited source support.
- **Research relevance:** HIGH as an archived non-mainline direction.

### N4 — Single A100 execution cannot run the frozen VLM semantic contract

- **Finding:** the frozen FP8 checkpoint materializes as BF16 on A100 SM8.0, consuming roughly 78–79 GiB and leaving no generation headroom. All attempts fail before inference and produce zero semantic outputs.
- **Evidence:** `outputs/v10_multiseal_reference_v1/SINGLE_GPU_FEASIBILITY_AUDIT.md`.
- **Research relevance:** LOW for paper novelty; HIGH for reproducibility/resource planning.

## Null results and unexercised semantics

- **Unknown bridge:** P0 selected only 6 unknown units total; no predicted unknown bridge occurs. `unknown != negative` is not experimentally established here.
- **Parse-failure barrier:** released reference has no parse-failure units. Its semantics are code-level only in this evaluation.
- **Duration cap:** C1→C2 median ΔF1 is `0`; 44/54 traces tie. It is a safe constraint but not a dominant P0 result.
- **K3 regressions:** no K3<K0 primary P0 cell exists, so there is no observed K0-favorable event regime; equality is common at low budget/when no useful positives are selected.

## Evaluation limitations

1. All primary unit outcomes and event references are Qwen model-relative. They are not human/official semantic labels.
2. Reference events use full-grid current-V3 K3 grouping. The P0 event-F1 advantage can therefore partly measure agreement with that grouping definition.
3. Only one query predicate is tested across the three videos.
4. P0 uses query-call budgets, not measured physical wall-clock/hard deadlines.
5. The comparison has three fair static selectors, not faithful reimplementations of every historical SUPG/ARC/ABae variant.
6. P0 has deterministic traces; bootstrap over selector×video×budget cells is descriptive, not a population-level randomized trial.

## Open hypotheses

- A sparse semantic event query needs an explicit temporal relation-materialization stage beyond predicate selection. **PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED.**
- A continuity/gap rule, rather than negative barriers, is the material contributor in the released V3 regime. **SUPPORTED for this model-relative P0 contract.**
- Materialization dominates selector choice. **INCONCLUSIVE:** median pairwise selector difference is smaller than materializer gain, but median per-cell selector spread is larger.
- The continuity finding agrees with independent human event continuity. **NOT TESTED.**
- The physical deadline executor has cross-video generalization. **NOT TESTED.**
