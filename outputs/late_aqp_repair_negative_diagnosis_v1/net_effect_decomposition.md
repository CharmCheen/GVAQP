## Key observation

Across 45 trials, D3-core has on average -0.8 fewer unique bins queried than D3-norepair, while using 0.8 repair calls and 21.8 duplicate discovery calls. The lost unique bins are the primary driver of lower event recall.

**Uncertainty note**: The 'lost exploration' estimate is heuristic. We count repair calls plus discovery duplicates as wasted budget, but we cannot exactly reconstruct which fresh bins would have been explored instead.
