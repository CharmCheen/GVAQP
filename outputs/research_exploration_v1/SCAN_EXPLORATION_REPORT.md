# SCAN exploration report

Decision: **REVISE** (candidate-generation gate not yet run for V3).

The V3 full-grid fail-stopped at 136/1,475 on a sealed per-call cost-bound
breach and cannot release a reference. The candidate universe, feature table,
reference join, and measured SCAN cost also do not exist. Therefore uniform,
random, raw-proxy, PSTR, ARC, current
SCAN, and full-legal-universe coverage cannot be compared on the frozen query.
Proposal event recall, uncovered V3 events, candidates/hour, and coverage over
SCAN wall time are all unmeasured.

Older cached evidence is diagnostic only. Raw-proxy scheduling beat the ARC
adaptation at most logical budgets, but PSTR remained stronger than raw proxy
over the budget curve. The cached data preserve no visual primitives, so an
event-value ranking model, shuffled-feature control, and ID-only control cannot
be validly fitted. This is neither a GO nor a mechanism rejection.

Minimum conclusion-changing experiment after a newly sealed V3 release: run the already
frozen SCAN once on all three videos, bind its physical timing and primitive
outputs, join every legal candidate to the released K3 relation evaluator-side,
and compare coverage curves for uniform, raw proxy, PSTR, ARC, current SCAN,
and the full legal universe. Do not train ranking unless the universe itself
has adequate multi-video event coverage.
