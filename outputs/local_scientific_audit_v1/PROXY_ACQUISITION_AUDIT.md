## 6. Miss taxonomy — do not conflate

| Type | Definition | Local status |
|---|---|---|
| RANKING_MISS | candidate exists but proxy ranks it poorly | VERIFIED: AUPRC 0.2584–0.3146; R3 gap 0.0951; low scores delay VERIFY |
| EXPOSURE_MISS | candidate/evidence not produced by cheap sensing | NOT MEASURED on the audited substrate (impossible by construction); synthetic stress only |
| SCHEDULING_RECOVERABLE_MISS | another legal acquisition action would expose the missed evidence | NOT MEASURED for natural misses; Guangzhou probe shows scheduling CAN change which candidate is verified in time (unit 346 at ~224 s) — single source, single event |
| SENSOR_SUPPORT_MISS | current sensing operator cannot expose it | NOT MEASURED; the physical pilot's trigger-based candidate generation exists but was never evaluated as a miss-measurement device |

None of these implies any other. In particular the R3 ranking result must not
be cited as evidence of natural exposure misses, and the Guangzhou scheduling
result (single source/event) must not be cited as evidence of a general
exposure-recoverability mechanism.
