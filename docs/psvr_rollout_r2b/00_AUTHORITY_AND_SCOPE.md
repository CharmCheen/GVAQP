# H-ROLLOUT1B authority and scope

H-ROLLOUT1A-R2 established an **ideal-mechanism** result only: under the frozen finite-support toy model, exact visible-history posterior, full-horizon continuation, and zero planning cost, M1 strictly improved on shielded pi0. The evidence is bound by the completed E1 marker and independent verifier.

H-ROLLOUT1B asks a different, falsifiable question: whether a fixed, computationally realizable approximate rollout has positive *net* utility after planning time under a predeclared region of estimator and world-model error. It does not modify or reopen the 1A decision.

This package is result-blind: no 1B seed universe, runner, approximate policy, trace, outcome, or policy comparison is created by this preregistration. Real video replay, physical Oracle calls, GPU extraction, learned M0, and physical safety claims remain outside scope.
