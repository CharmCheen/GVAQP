# Selected-Frontier Conversion Calibration Contract V1

Status: `FROZEN_INPUT_GATE_PENDING`  
Research problem: `SELECTED_FRONTIER_CONVERSION_CALIBRATION`  
Primary blocker: `SELECTED_FRONTIER_CONVERSION_CALIBRATION`

## Purpose and frozen system

The sole objective is to determine whether the candidate actually selected by
the frozen Frontier under R4 can be assigned a cross-video calibrated
probability of creating one new distinct confirmed event after complete atomic
CONFIRM. This contract does not evaluate, tune, or reopen any adaptive
SCAN/CONFIRM controller.

The selected operational controller remains `R4_FIXED_TIME_RATIO_25_75`:
`ANYTIME_LARGEST_GAP` SCAN, 25% realized complete-action time for SCAN, 75% for
CONFIRM, frozen Y8 candidate generator, capacity-10 score Frontier, highest-score
eligible CONFIRM target, frozen Oracle/parser, K3/materialization,
deduplication, durable commit, and conservative deadline admission.

`MYOPIC_VPS_V1=REJECTED_UTILITY_SCALE_MISMATCH` and
`COMMON_UTILITY_ALIGNMENT_V1=NOT_ESTABLISHED`. Ratio-Anchored is implemented
but not authorized for evaluation. Bandits and SMDPs remain deferred; RL is
prohibited.

## Sample and label

One primary sample is a real pre-CONFIRM decision state
`x_t=(s_t,c_top_t)` generated by a completed physical R4 execution. `c_top_t`
is exactly the witness selected by the frozen Frontier. The binary label is:

`y_t = 1[complete CONFIRM(c_top_t), including parse, materialize, deduplicate,
and durable commit, adds a previously uncommitted distinct event before the
deadline]`.

An ordinary candidate Oracle label, event membership, positive semantic label,
or full candidate-pool conversion is not a substitute. Duplicate committed
events, materialization failures, post-deadline results, incomplete actions, and
positive candidates that add no distinct event all receive `y=0` or are marked
invalid according to the atomic-action contract. No post-deadline sample enters
the primary calibration endpoint.

The unique sample identity hashes video source, query, independently reset run,
public pre-action state, committed-event history, Frontier snapshot, and top
candidate. Repeated identical states across budgets or retries count once for
model fitting. Physical repetitions may estimate cost variation but do not
inflate the statistical sample count.

## Input-video gate and blind inclusion

The two existing videos V0 (`long_video_dataset3`) and V1 (`驾驶-大理`) are
design-only and cannot satisfy the new-video requirement or the primary
cross-video gate.

At least four new, mutually independent, complete source videos are required:

- at least three calibration/validation videos;
- at least one separately sealed test video;
- each from a distinct non-overlapping capture session;
- forward-facing road view compatible with the frozen queries;
- minimum 1,200 seconds, recommended at least 1,800 seconds;
- stable start/middle/tail and full sequential decode;
- no crop, reencode, normalization, split, overlap, or derivative of an
  existing/design/new video as an independent source;
- hash-bound source declaration with a unique capture session ID.

Files are selected before any query label, reference, candidate, event density,
informative-state count, calibration result, or method result is opened. The
deterministic order is earliest valid inventory registration time, then source
filename lexical order. Once eligible, a video cannot be replaced because of
low event density, few positive labels, unfavorable calibration, or technical
cost unless a preregistered technical gate fails. Exact duplicates and known or
content-demonstrated derivatives are grouped as one source and only the
technically valid original representation may remain.

Each file must have `<filename>.source.json` containing:

```json
{
  "source_kind": "original_continuous_capture",
  "capture_session_id": "nonempty globally unique ID",
  "registration_utc": "ISO-8601 UTC timestamp fixed when first inventoried",
  "known_parent_video": null,
  "known_derivation_operation": null,
  "target_event_information_used_for_selection": false
}
```

Provider attestation is admissible for provenance but cannot override direct
duplicate, overlap, decode, duration, or timeline evidence. Test-role assignment
is frozen by taking the fourth eligible new source in deterministic order; its
semantic outputs remain sealed until every model and threshold is frozen.
For assets already inventoried before this contract, a hash-bound prior audit or
attestation date is the immutable legacy registration time; submissions made
after this contract must provide `registration_utc` directly in the sidecar.

If fewer than four new sources pass, the benchmark is
`BLOCKED_INSUFFICIENT_INDEPENDENT_VIDEOS`. Only technical/provenance audits and
an exact acquisition request may continue. No Oracle call, proxy extraction,
model fit, calibration metric, or controller replay is allowed.

## Frozen R4 data generation

After the input gate and explicit authorization for substantial compute, every
new calibration/validation video/query group is run with the frozen R4 policy
at independently reset 30, 60, 120, and 240 second deadlines. Identical state
samples are deduplicated by the frozen identity hash. The sealed test video is
executed once only after the formal model freeze.

Immediately before every legal CONFIRM the durable ledger records only causal
public information:

- remaining budget and conservative complete CONFIRM cost;
- coverage fraction, maximum gap, completed SCAN count/time;
- Frontier size, top/min/median/max/quantile scores and occupancy;
- top-candidate age and cluster novelty;
- committed distinct-event/cluster state summary;
- recent causal SCAN yield and CONFIRM conversion history;
- deadline admission decision;
- after completion: atomic outcome, `y`, actual complete cost, commit token,
  deadline status, and state hash.

Query ID is retained only for grouping and leave-one-query-out reporting in V1;
it is not a model feature or independent bucket. Video ID, filename, source
dataset ID, reference events, future candidates/outcomes/costs, total event
count, and held-out results are forbidden model inputs.

## Informative-state accounting

For any state with complete evaluator-only counterfactual action outcomes,
`I_t=1` iff the true aligned one-step utility of SCAN and CONFIRM differs.
Report total decision states, both-legal states, informative states, informative
groups, direction by group, selected-Frontier conversion positives, and the
fraction of states lacking counterfactual support.

R4 observational data alone identifies selected-CONFIRM conversion but not the
unchosen SCAN counterfactual. Such states are marked
`INFORMATIVE_STATUS=NOT_IDENTIFIED_MISSING_COUNTERFACTUAL`, never silently
treated as ties. Informative-state counts cannot be used as the calibration
sample size and cannot be manufactured by action-row resampling.

## Model space

Only these preregistered models are permitted:

- C0: constant selected-Frontier conversion rate;
- C1: score-bin Beta–Bernoulli with global shrinkage;
- C2: age × score hierarchical shrinkage with global and score fallback;
- C3: L2-regularized logistic regression on the frozen public numeric fields;
- C4: shallow LightGBM diagnostic upper bound, depth at most 3, at most 50
  trees, no role in model selection if dependency or support is unavailable.

No query-specific model/bin is allowed. Hyperparameters are fixed defaults or
nested within training videos only; the test video is never used. C0 is fitted
within each training fold and is the primary comparator. C4 cannot rescue a
failed calibration gate by ranking alone.

## Evaluation and statistics

Primary metric: `Selected-Frontier Brier Score` under leave-one-video-out
(LOVO). Secondary metrics: log loss, calibration intercept/slope, reliability
table/diagram, AUPRC, top-score-bin conversion, leave-one-query-out diagnostics,
per-group delta, group win/nonnegative rate, 10,000 paired video-level bootstrap,
leave-best-group-out, positive count, effective sample size, and missingness.

Samples are not bootstrapped as independent observations. Video is the primary
independent unit; video/query is a reported paired subgroup. Class-absent folds
retain Brier/log-loss but mark AUPRC and calibration slope unavailable.

## Calibration gate

At least one nonconstant model passes only if all are true:

1. pooled LOVO Brier is lower than fold-fitted C0;
2. pooled LOVO log loss is lower than C0;
3. a strict majority of independent videos have nonnegative Brier delta;
4. leave-best-video-out mean Brier improvement remains positive;
5. calibration slope is finite and positive where identifiable;
6. selected-Frontier positives occur in at least three independent videos;
7. at least 100 unique selected-Frontier samples and at least 20 positives exist;
8. no leakage, invalid commit, deadline overrun, or post-deadline label enters;
9. no single video contributes 50% or more of total positive labels.

If the gate fails, `SELECTED_FRONTIER_CONVERSION_CALIBRATION=NOT_ESTABLISHED`,
R4 remains selected, and Static Common-Utility/Ratio-Anchored/controller replay
remain blocked. Passing this gate authorizes only a new static common-utility
contract, not controller deployment.

## Deadline cost calibration

Physical runs require a calibration-video set disjoint from the sealed final
test. Complete SCAN and CONFIRM costs use a frozen split-conformal upper bound:

`cbar(a,x)=qhat_(1-alpha)(c(a)|x)+q_calibration`, with `alpha=0.01`.

If conditional support is insufficient, fall back to the global complete-action
bound. The physical gate requires zero deadline overrun, post-deadline commit,
and incomplete action. Development maximum +0.25 seconds remains a replay-only
diagnostic and cannot support a physical safety claim.

## Compute and Oracle authorization

This contract explicitly permits new physical Oracle calls only after the four-
video input gate passes, source roles and hashes are frozen, and the user grants
explicit authorization for the estimated substantial GPU/Oracle budget. Before
that authorization, all semantic extraction is prohibited.

## Deliverables and stopping

Outputs live under `outputs/selected_frontier_conversion_v1/`: contracts,
input audit, sample ledger, calibration folds/predictions/metrics, cost
calibration, reports, repair log, environment/code versions, and artifact hashes.

Stop for fewer than four eligible new videos, source-independence uncertainty,
incomplete labels/commits, insufficient positive/video support, leakage, failed
calibration gate, or exhausted single repair cycle. Failure delivers R4 and the
minimal acquisition/unlock condition; it never reopens Ratio-Anchored, Bandit,
SMDP, or RL.

This contract is immutable after its SHA-256 enters the freeze manifest.
