# Closed and weakened branches

Closed means “do not invest further on the current evidence and substrate,”
not a universal impossibility theorem. Negative artifacts remain part of the
record.

| Branch | Status | Decisive reason | Reopen condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| Coverage-Debt controller | **NO-GO / CLOSED** | No stable cross-video quality mechanism in the frozen workload. | A new faithful substrate independently shows material, replicated failure specifically addressed by coverage debt. |
| Dynamic V3 controller | **NO-GO / CLOSED** | Fixed SCAN1→VERIFY1 is stronger in the cached study; adaptive calibration does not transfer. | P4 establishes predictable physical action regret with held-out gain over the fixed default. |
| MAB selector | **NO-GO / CLOSED** | 0/18 immediate positives, 1/7 timing-only continuation conversion, near-random public-state prediction, worse regret than fixed action, and poor structural fit. | No direct reopen. A successful P4 would justify reassessing the decision class, not presupposing MAB. |
| Selective-deviation classifier | **NO-GO / CLOSED** | On 108 sampled cached states, learned rules do not generalize and fixed always-VERIFY has far lower regret. | Prospective, population-weighted, multi-video physical state data with a reproducible legal predictor. |
| Complex K3 as main novelty | **NO-GO / CLOSED** | Gap-only C1 explains nearly all observed gain; duration, negative barrier, and K3 extras have zero median increment. | Independent-human data reveal a specific failure that C1 cannot handle and a complex component independently fixes. |
| `number_of_temporal_regions_touched` as a universal utility surrogate | **DISFAVORED / WEAKENED** | VLM shadow has only 2/6 positive clusters, conflicting proxy directions, and negligible macro/LOVO gain. | Human P1 shows stable cross-cluster, cross-proxy equal-yield benefit. |
| Geometry-aware policy escalation | **NO-GO / CLOSED FOR NOW** | Human effect unresolved; shadow potential LOW; existing model-relative P2 is inconclusive and semantic-state residual absent. | Human P1 PASS, followed by a gated killer showing residual legal headroom. |
| Existing model-relative P2 as authorization for P3 | **NO-GO / CLOSED** | It ran without resolved human P1 and explicitly prohibits P3; its results are diagnostic only. | It cannot itself authorize P3. A new authorization must follow the state machine. |
| Learned controller from current 108-state table | **NO-GO / CLOSED** | Sample is not natural state prevalence, costs are abstract, utility is model-relative, and two precision-boundary crossings dominate positive value. | Complete faithful P4 prevalence/action-regret audit first. |

Retained bounded defaults/findings:

- fixed SCAN1→VERIFY1 is the strong current sequential default, not a universal
  optimum;
- C1 gap continuity is the minimal retained materializer, not a strong novelty
  claim;
- StaticProxyRank is the strongest robust current policy in the frozen
  model-relative query-budget matrix, not a human-validated general solution.
