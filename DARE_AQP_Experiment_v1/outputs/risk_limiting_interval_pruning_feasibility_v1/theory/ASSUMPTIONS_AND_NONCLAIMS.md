# Assumptions and nonclaims

## Assumptions

- canonical anchors uniquely assign events to disjoint 10-second units;
- provisional frontier blocks are disjoint;
- audit inclusion probabilities are known and generated independently of
  hidden block outcomes conditional on frozen ANY output/history;
- fully auditing a sampled block costs and identifies every child unit exactly;
- interval cost follows only the registered C0 families.

## Nonclaims

No physical operator accuracy, human-event validity, cross-video validity,
parametric error law, error independence, or production sequential guarantee is
claimed. Synthetic placements are falsification tests, not a generative model.
Empirical coverage supplements but does not replace the conservative bound.
